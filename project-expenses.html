Expense Tracker

A simple way to record money coming in and going out, then make the totals easier to understand.

Project overview

The application concept centers on recording income and expenses, assigning descriptions or categories, calculating the balance, and eventually presenting summaries or budgets. The Nexus Lab currently includes a small browser-based transaction demo to explore the basic interaction.

Design goals

  • Make adding a transaction quick and clear.
  • Keep income, spending, and balance distinct.
  • Validate entries and handle empty states.
  • Build reports only after transaction handling is reliable.

TECHNOLOGY AREAS

HTMLCSSJavaScriptData visualization
Project status

Concept with a working small browser demo in Nexus Lab. A full account-based or server-backed product would require additional development.
